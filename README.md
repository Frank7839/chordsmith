# Chordsmith Studio

**A free songwriting studio for musicians.** Find the chords in any key, build a progression, and write lyrics with the chords placed on the right words.

**Open the app: https://frank7839.github.io/chordsmith/**

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your songs are saved only on your own device.

---

## Features

**Find the right chords**
- Every chord in any key and mode: major, minor, Dorian, Mixolydian, Lydian and Phrygian
- Colour-coded chord roles: home, moving away, pulling back
- Borrowed chords from the parallel key
- Interactive circle of fifths with your key's chord family and closely related keys

**Build your song**
- Separate progressions for verse, chorus, bridge and more
- Loop a section or play the whole song, with a click track and three sounds
- Classic progressions to start from, or "Surprise me"
- One-tap transpose
- As many songs as you like

**Write lyrics that fit**
- Chord sheet with each chord above the right word, on the beat
- Play along: the current chord lights up and the sheet scrolls with the song
- Rhyme and syllable checks for English, Hindi and Telugu lyrics

**Play it on your instrument**
- Guitar diagrams with a capo helper
- Ukulele (standard shapes first), mandolin, bass and 17-key kalimba
- Piano voicings with smooth voice leading
- Note names as letters, Hindustani sargam or Carnatic swaras

## Install it like an app

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, then tap **Install app** at the top (or ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**)
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, then tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**
- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** click the install icon in the address bar

Once installed, it works offline.
